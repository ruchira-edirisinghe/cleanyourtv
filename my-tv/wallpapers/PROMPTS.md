# Wallpaper prompts

The idle wallpapers behind the immersive Home, made with Higgsfield GPT Image 2.5 (16:9, 2k, high quality), then saved
as 1920x1080 JPEGs. The launcher rotates through the theme folder's `wallpapers/` and moves to the next one each
time the TV is switched on.

## 01-lake-dusk

A still alpine lake at dusk below a snow-dusted mountain, its perfect reflection in glassy water, dark pine forest along the shore, soft lavender and peach sky. Ultra-wide calm landscape photograph for a TV lock-screen wallpaper, in the style of Apple's macOS and tvOS landscape wallpapers. Composition: soft open sky fills the upper and middle part of the frame with no strong detail behind the centre, where a large clock will sit; the horizon and main landforms sit in the lower half; the bottom fifth falls into deep, quiet shadow. Muted, natural, cinematic colour grading, gentle atmospheric haze, ultra sharp, 16:9. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, people, animals, buildings or user interface.
